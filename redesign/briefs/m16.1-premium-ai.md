## 0. The scene, and what M16 does to it

Ten friends in voice. A lobby opens, teams appear, they play, ratings move. Nobody typed anything. **M16 adds
no step to that scene.** For a Premium group, a line of story appears under the result a minute after the
game, a paragraph opens the Sunday post, and each regular's page has a short scouting report. Nobody asks for
them, nobody approves them, nobody rates them. For every other group, nothing about the product changes.

AI is a writer here, never a source. Every number it prints is one the database already holds, and a checker
proves that before anything is shown. A line that fails the check is no line.

## 1. Framing

### 1.1 What a group without Premium sees

**Nothing.** No upsell, no banner, no nag, no greyed toggle, no locked card, no empty slot, no layout gap
where a line would be, no `Premium` badge on other groups' pages, no mention on `/`, `/about`, `/how` or
`/download`. The word `Premium` appears nowhere a member of a non-Premium group can reach. Their pages render
byte-for-byte as they do today (M16.2 acceptance pins this).

Kustom is still free, and `00-product.md`'s "Pricing. Kustom is free." stays true: Premium is not sold, it is
switched on by the operator. Selling it is a future decision row.

### 1.2 What a Premium group sees, surface by surface

The friends in a Premium group never see the word `Premium` either. They see AI-written lines, each carrying
its label (1.3). The word `Premium` appears only to that group's admins and owner, as the heading of the one
admin section where the AI off switch lives, and to the operator on `/ops`.

| Surface | What appears | Where exactly | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discord result post** | The game's recap line | Added to the existing result post by an edit when the line lands, if it lands within 15 minutes of the post. Last block of the post, under the result and rating changes. | Delaying the post; a second, separate message; an edit after 15 minutes |
| **Tonight** | The same recap line | On the finished-game poster, under the result headline and the `Upset!` tag, above the rating changes. Appears live when it lands (the page already updates itself). Not repeated in the folded log of earlier games. | Inside the receipt; anywhere in the open, balanced or in-game states (nothing is generated before or during a game) |
| **Game page** | The same recap line | Under the game header, above the receipt and the scoreboard. | Inside the receipt or the scoreboard |
| **Player page** | The scouting report, two or three lines | Under the header block (big Rating, settling chip, trend line, `Started at 1200…` line), above the games list. All windows show the same report; it says which week it was written (`Written Sunday 4 Oct`). | On a settling player (fewer than 10 rated games); on an opted-out player; on the You page's admin part |
| **Sunday Discord post** | The weekly storyline, one paragraph | The first block of the existing weekly post, above the final board and the three awards. | In the monthly post (out of M16); replacing or rewording any award or board line |
| **Board, Last week** | The same storyline | Under the window header, above the list, on `Last week` only. Gone when the next week closes and that week becomes Last week. | On This week, the months or All time |

When a line is missing for any reason (not generated yet, rejected, over budget, API down, opted out, group
switched off), the surface looks exactly as it does for a non-Premium group. No placeholder, no
`Recap unavailable`, no spinner.

### 1.3 The label

The 2026-10-03 row named the label `recap`. **Changed openly (decision row D3):** a bare `recap` does not say
a machine wrote it, and the point of the label is that a friend never mistakes it for a fact Kustom measured.

- Game line and weekly storyline: `AI recap` [NEW COPY]
- Player page: `AI scouting report` [NEW COPY]
- Tapping or hovering the label, on the site: `Written by AI from the numbers on this page. Every number in
  it is checked against the game before it shows up.` [NEW COPY]
  - The weekly storyline's version: `Written by AI from this week's games. Every number in it is checked
    against the board before it shows up.` [NEW COPY]
  - The scouting report's version: `Written by AI from this player's games. Every number in it is checked
    before it shows up. Rewritten every Sunday.` [NEW COPY]
- Discord: the line is preceded by `AI recap` as a small label (Discord subtext or the block's title; the
  designer's call) and carries no link or mention.

Visual weight is the designer's (`05-design.md`), with two product constraints: it must look like a caption,
quieter than any number on the surface, and it must not reuse the receipt's styling.

### 1.4 Opt-out: the player

Any player in a Premium group can ask not to be written about. It is per group (every group is its own
world, and Premium is per group). Default: written about, because the group's admins asked for Premium and
the operator turned it on.

**Where:** the You page, signed in, only in a Premium group with AI lines on. One switch.

- Heading: `AI lines about you` [NEW COPY]
- Body: `This group has short AI-written lines about each game, a paragraph about the week, and a few lines on
  each player's page, all built from the numbers Kustom already has. Turn this off and none of them will name
  you or describe you. Your games and your rating don't change.` [NEW COPY]
- Switch label: `Write about me` [NEW COPY], on by default.
- After turning it off: `Done. Kustom won't write about you in <Group name>.` [NEW COPY]
- After turning it back on: `Done. You'll show up in AI lines again from the next game.` [NEW COPY]

**For a friend who never signs in** (most of them): an admin can switch it off for them, from that member's
row in the admin member list: `Don't write about <Name>` [NEW COPY], confirming
`<Name> won't be named in AI lines.` [NEW COPY]. An admin can only switch it **off**, never back on; only the
player switches themselves back on. Asking an admin in the group chat is not a night step.

**What opting out does:**
- From that moment, the player is left out of every fact list, so no new line can name or describe them.
  Team totals still count their numbers; the model just has no handle for them.
- Lines already stored that name them stop showing on the site at once (checked at render, not by deleting).
- Their scouting report stops showing and is not regenerated.
- Discord posts already sent stay as sent: Kustom cannot unsend them. The You page body does not promise it.

### 1.5 Opt-out: the group

A Premium group's admins and owner can switch AI lines off for the whole group, in the admin home.

- Section heading: `Kustom Premium` [NEW COPY]
- Row: `AI lines` [NEW COPY] with an on/off switch, on when Premium is first switched on.
- Under it, on: `Short AI-written lines on each game, the Sunday post and player pages. Every number is
  checked against your group's games before it shows up.` [NEW COPY]
- Under it, off: `Off. No new lines are written, and the ones already written are hidden. Turn it back on and
  they come back.` [NEW COPY]
- Budget line, only when the month's cap is reached: `AI lines are paused until <1 Nov>: this month's budget
  is used up. Everything else works as usual.` [NEW COPY]
- Members and visitors see none of this section. No confirm on the switch: off loses nothing.

**Hiding one line.** On the game page and the player page, an admin sees `Hide` [NEW COPY] beside the label;
one tap, no confirm, and the line is gone for everybody (`Hidden. It won't come back.` [NEW COPY]). This is
the escape hatch for a line that passed the checker and still reads badly. Members never see it. A hidden line
is counted in M16.7's review. (Decision row D8.)

## 2. Entitlement model (M16.2)

- **One flag per group, default off.** Product requirements for the migration (platform writes it, shows the
  user before applying):
  - `groups.premium boolean not null default false`
  - `groups.premium_changed_at timestamptz null`, set whenever the flag changes, so `/ops` can say since when.
  - No plan, tier, price, expiry, seat count or billing column. Those belong to the future billing decision
    and would only be guesses now.
  - Every existing group, `customs` included, is off after the migration. Turning `customs` on is a separate,
    deliberate operator step (question 3).
- **Who turns it on: the operator only, by a service-role script, not by a route.** M16.2's text said
  "set only by a super-admin (M14.19's `/ops`)". But M13.6 decided the super-admin is **read-only, no admin
  write**, and M14.19 kept that. A write button on `/ops` would be the first super-admin write. So
  (decision D2):
  - `pnpm --filter web set-premium <slug> on|off` reads `apps/web/.env.local` like `mint-token`, writes the
    flag with the service role, prints the group's name and the new state, and is idempotent.
  - **No HTTP route writes `groups.premium`.** Any group-settings route that receives a `premium` field
    ignores it or refuses it, for owner, admin, member, signed-out and super-admin alike.
  - `/ops` shows the flag and `premium_changed_at` read-only beside each group, plus that group's AI spend
    this month against its cap.
- **What the flag gates:** every M16 generator (no model call when off) and every M16 render (no line when
  off), checked server-side. Turning Premium off hides stored lines exactly as the group off switch does; it
  deletes nothing.
- **M16.3's migration** (separate, also shown first) adds, as product requirements:
  - `groups.ai_lines_enabled boolean not null default true` (the admin switch in 1.5)
  - `groups.ai_monthly_cap_usd numeric not null default 2.00` (operator-set, by the same script with
    `--cap <usd>`; no route)
  - a per-player-per-group opt-out (`group_memberships.ai_opt_out boolean not null default false`, keyed by
    the membership, which is keyed by player, which is keyed by PUUID); the admin path can only set it true
  - the lines table: one row per `(group_id, kind, subject)` with `kind` in `game | week | player`, subject
    being the game id, the week start, or the player plus week start; unique on that triple. Stores the
    text with `P` tokens (not names), the token-to-player map, the fact list, model, prompt version, status
    (`published | rejected | hidden`), the reject reason, tokens in and out, and cost. Rejected rows are kept
    (they make generation idempotent and feed M16.7's rejection rate).

## 3. Model and cost

### 3.1 Models

**Superseded 2026-10-04 (product, M16.7).** Every feature now runs on `claude-sonnet-5-5`. The game recap line
moved in M16.8 (decision row "The game recap line moves to Sonnet 5.5 (M16.8)": Haiku 4.5 wrote box scores,
2.8 of 5 against 4.2), and the scouting report moved in M16.9 (its done note in `docs/02-milestones.md`). The
Haiku 4.5 cells below, and the Haiku prices in 3.2, are kept only as the original plan. Sonnet 5.5's real price
is $2 / $10 per million tokens (decision row "AI models", 2026-10-04). M16.7's reviewer measured about $0.94 a
group a month on a synthetic month, 47% of the $2 cap.

| Feature | Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Game recap line | ~~`claude-haiku-4-5`~~ `claude-sonnet-5-5` (M16.8) | One short line, five a night; volume and latency matter more than prose |
| Weekly storyline | `claude-sonnet-5-5` | One paragraph a week; the one place prose quality is the whole point |
| Scouting report | ~~`claude-haiku-4-5`~~ `claude-sonnet-5-5` (M16.9) | Twenty-ish short reports a week from tight per-player facts |

Model ids live in one config next to the prompts, so a newer model is a one-line change plus a fixture run
through the checker. No model is ever chosen per request at runtime.

### 3.2 Cost estimate, one group of about 10 regulars playing nightly

Assumptions: 5 games a night, 30 nights a month (150 games), 20 players with a scouting report, 4.3 weeks a
month, and a 1.2x factor for the one allowed retry (section 4.5). Prices: Haiku 4.5 at $1 / $5 per million
tokens in / out; Sonnet 5.5 estimated conservatively at $3 / $15 (published figures as low as $2 / $10 have
been reported; M16.3 records the real price beside the cap, per the milestone).

| Feature | Per unit | Units a month | Per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recap line | ~2,000 in + ~80 out = **$0.0024 a game** | 150 x 1.2 | **~$0.43** |
| Weekly storyline | ~8,000 in + ~400 out = **$0.030 a week** | 4.3 x 1.2 | **~$0.16** |
| Scouting report | ~3,000 in + ~120 out = **$0.0036 a player-week**, ~$0.07 a week | 86 x 1.2 | **~$0.37** |
| **Total** | | | **~$0.96 a group a month** |

### 3.3 Budget cap, and what happens at it

- **Per group: $2.00 a month** (about twice the estimate), operator-adjustable per group. The month is the
  calendar month on the group clock the product already uses.
- **Global: $20 a month** across every group, as a server setting (enough for about 20 Premium groups at the
  estimate), plus a **spend limit the user sets in the Anthropic console** at the same figure, so a bug in our
  meter still cannot spend past it.
- **A call is made only if the remaining budget covers its worst case** (its `max_tokens` priced as output
  plus its input), so the cap is never overshot by the call that crosses it.
- **At the cap:** that group's features go quiet until the 1st of next month. No line, no friend-facing
  message, no Discord notice. Everything else in the product is untouched. The admin home shows the one budget
  line in 1.5; `/ops` shows the group as capped. At the global cap, every group goes quiet the same way.
- **Kill switch:** one operator setting that stops every model call for every group within five minutes,
  without a code change or a deploy (platform picks the mechanism). Removing `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` must also
  stop everything cleanly, never crash ingest or a post.

## 4. Guardrails, made concrete

These sharpen guardrails 1 to 6 in the milestone; none softens one.

### 4.1 Facts only from our data

- A **fact builder** (one module, all model calls behind it, the way `packages/lcu` holds the client) turns
  stored rows into a numbered list: `F3: P4 | champion Lee Sin | kills 9 | assists 5 | team kills 19 |
  kills+assists share 14 of 19`. Derived numbers (shares, streaks, climbs, win rates) are computed by the
  server and appear in the facts; the model never does arithmetic.
- Allowed sources: `game_players` stats, champion, role, side, win, duration, the `Upset` flag, stored
  head-to-head and duo counts, the week's board and awards as the Sunday post prints them, and per-player
  role and champion counts.
- **Not in any fact list:** win chance or odds (the receipt owns fairness, principle 3; the line may say the
  underdog won, from the `Upset` flag, and nothing more), MVP and ACE (the poster says them; restating invites
  drift if a formula changes), rating numbers on the game recap (a rebuild can change them; stored lines must
  stay true), ranks from solo queue, anything from another group, anything from the live game (no
  teamfights, Barons, dragons, minutes other than the final duration).
- Games that get a recap: games ingested live by a host while the group is Premium with AI lines on, Rift or
  ARAM. Not backfilled or recovered games, not games from before Premium was switched on (no retroactive
  run). ARAM's line shows on the game page and Tonight only, since Discord posts no ARAM result.
- Scouting report facts: only for players with 10 or more rated games in the group; a champion or role
  figure only when it covers 5 or more games; regenerated on Sunday only for players who played that week
  (otherwise last week's stays). Describes, never prescribes causes.
- Weekly storyline facts: the closed week's board order and week Rating climbs, records, streaks and the
  three awards, exactly as the Sunday post computes them. The storyline is a snapshot of that Sunday and is
  never rewritten.

### 4.2 Names and privacy

- Players go to the model as `P1..Pn` only. No Riot ID, game name, tag, PUUID, Discord name, group name or
  group link is ever in a request body. Champion names are fine (public game content).
- The model writes `{P4}`; after the check, the server substitutes the display name the rest of the site
  uses for that player. The stored line keeps the tokens, so a rename shows the new name on the site.
  Discord gets the name at the time of the edit.
- Names inserted into Discord are escaped and sent with mentions disabled, so a name can never ping or
  inject markdown.
- Prompts carry no free text from players. There is no field anyone types into that reaches a model.
- English only.

### 4.3 Friendly, never mean

The tone is a friend at the table who was watching: warm, specific, a little teasing. It is never a roast
of a person. The rules are **structural first, prompt second**, because a prompt is a hope and a fact list is
a fence:

1. **Tease up, never down.** Only the winning side's players can be the subject of a tease. The fact builder
   gives the losing side's players only their best numbers (top damage, kills, vision, CS), never deaths or a
   low stat. The model has no material to mock someone who lost, and the checker rejects any number not in the
   facts.
2. **The game, never the person.** No line mentions rank, rating loss, skill as a person, real life, looks,
   age, nationality, or anything outside that game's or week's numbers. The prompt says so; a deny-list of
   insults, slurs and personal terms rejects the line outright.
3. **No spicy mode.** The research's spicy tone (idea 7) is out of M16. Teasing stays at the level of
   `Sami can stop farming now, Blue won.`
4. **Escape hatch.** An admin's one-tap `Hide` (1.5), and the group switch.

### 4.4 The checker, before anything is published

Deterministic code, no second model. A line is published only if every check passes:

1. **Every number** (digits, percentages, `x of y`, durations, ordinals, and number words like `four`,
   `twice`, `9th`) matches a value in the facts, **bound to the right fact**: the number's sentence must
   contain the fact's player token (if the fact has one), and the fact's unit word (`kills`, `games`, `CS`)
   or a listed synonym must sit next to it. A 14 that is P5's kills cannot be printed as P3's.
2. **Every player reference** is a `{Pn}` token present in the facts. Any capitalised word not at a sentence
   start that is not a known champion (the pinned Data Dragon list), `Blue`, `Red`, `Kustom`, a role name or a
   weekday is rejected as a possible invented name.
3. **Every champion named** is in the facts for that subject.
4. **Absolute words** (`always`, `never`, `every`, `only`, `most`, `best`, `worst`, `least`, `first`) appear
   only in a sentence whose player token has a fact carrying that same word (the fact builder writes
   superlatives as facts, e.g. `F7: P3 | most CS per minute among mids in the group | 7.9`).
5. **Forbidden vocabulary:** causal claims (`because`, `due to`, `cost them`, `thanks to`), events we cannot
   see (`Baron`, `dragon`, `Herald`, `teamfight`, `gank`, `steal`, `throw`, `comeback`, `first blood`),
   odds words (`favored`, `chance`, `percent` tied to a side), and the insult deny-list.
6. **Shape:** recap one sentence or two, at most 220 characters; storyline at most 600 characters; scouting
   report two or three sentences, at most 300 characters. No links, no emoji, no markdown.

A rejected line is stored as `rejected` with the reason and is never shown anywhere.

### 4.5 Write once, cache, retry

- Generation is keyed by `(group, kind, subject)` and idempotent like ingest: a second companion's ingest,
  a page view, a re-render or a rebuild never calls the model again.
- **At most two model attempts per subject** (the second gets the checker's reason). Two rejections store
  `rejected` and that subject is finished for good. A transient API error (timeout, 5xx, rate limit) retries
  for up to 10 minutes for a game, a few hours for the weekly jobs, then gives up silently.
- Never generated on a page view (M16.6 acceptance).
- A rating rebuild or reset rewrites nothing.

### 4.6 Failure is silent absence

API down, key missing, cap reached, kill switch on, checker rejection, opted-out subject, group off: every
one renders exactly like a non-Premium group, logs one line server-side, and never delays or fails ingest, the
result post, the Sunday post or a page. No friend-facing error string exists for M16, on purpose.

## 5. Edge cases

| Case | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Fewer than ten in the game | Facts carry `P1..Pn` for whoever played; the line is about that game. |
| More than ten in the lobby | Sitters were not in the game and are not in its facts. The weekly storyline may mention a sitter only through week facts. |
| Someone leaves mid-lobby | No effect: lines come from the end-of-game block only. |
| Host disconnects before the end screen | The game arrives later by backfill: no recap (4.1). |
| Unknown or first-game player | A token like any other; the display name is whatever the game page shows. No scouting report (settling). |
| Opted-out player in the game | Left out of the facts; the line can still talk about the others; team totals still include them. |
| Player removed from the group | Their name stays on history, so stored lines naming them stay. Scouting report stops. |
| Player renamed | The site shows the new name (tokens); a Discord edit already made shows the old. |
| Two hosts in one game | One ingest, one line (idempotent). |
| Premium switched off then on | Stored lines come back; games played while off never get a line. |
| Line lands after 15 minutes | Game page and Tonight only; the Discord post is not edited. |

## 9. Acceptance, M16.2 to M16.7, as each should read after these decisions

- **M16.2 Premium entitlement.** Migration adds `groups.premium` (default false) and `premium_changed_at`,
  shown to the user first; every existing group reads off after it (test). `set-premium <slug> on|off` flips
  it, is idempotent, and stamps `premium_changed_at` (test). **No route writes it:** a request carrying

  super-admin (tests). `/ops` shows it read-only. A non-Premium group's Tonight, game page, player page, board,

  `Premium` and `AI recap` appear in none of them (test).
- **M16.3 Plumbing.** Every model call goes through one module; nothing else imports the Anthropic SDK or
  calls its host (lint or test). The checker rejects fixture lines with: one wrong number, one number bound to
  the wrong player, one spelled-out wrong number, one unknown name, one invented champion, one absolute word
  without a matching fact, one `because`, one `Baron`, one odds claim, one insult (one test each), and passes a
  clean fixture. A rejected line is stored `rejected` and shows nowhere (test). No request body contains a
  name, Riot ID, PUUID, Discord name or group name (test over every prompt builder). Losing-side facts carry
  no deaths or low stat (test). The per-group cap and the global cap each stop calls at the limit, and a call

  missing key each stop all calls without failing ingest (tests). Opted-out players are absent from every fact
  list, and a stored line naming one is not rendered (tests). The group switch off means no call and no render
  (test). The admin-only opt-out path can set true and never false (test). Real prices recorded beside the cap.
- **M16.4 Game recap line.** One line per live-ingested game while Premium and AI lines are on; none for
  backfilled games or games from before Premium (tests). Generated after ingest; the Discord result post goes
  out at today's timing without waiting (test), and is edited to add the labelled line only if it lands within
  15 minutes (test with a fake clock). Tonight's poster and the game page show it when it lands, under the
  result and above the receipt, never inside it (component tests). Idempotent per game: a second ingest makes
  no call (test). Discord names escaped with mentions off (test). Admin `Hide` removes it everywhere and
  members cannot see the control (tests).
- **M16.5 Weekly storyline.** One `claude-sonnet-5-5` call per group-week, at most two attempts, stored
  (test). It opens the Sunday post when present; the post is byte-identical to today's when it is missing
  (snapshot test). Shown on the board's Last week only (test). No rating rebuild rewrites it (test). Checker
  and opt-outs as M16.3.
- **M16.6 Scouting report.** Generated on the Sunday job for players with 10+ rated games who played that
  week, never on a page view (test: rendering a player page makes no call). None for settling or opted-out
  players (tests). Champion and role figures only from 5+ games (fact builder test). Placed under the header
  block with `AI scouting report` and its written date (component test). Describes, never prescribes: causal
  vocabulary is rejected (checker test).
- **M16.7 Review.** Reviewer walks guardrails 1 to 6 and sections 4.1 to 4.6 of this brief against the code;
  a month of fixture games (150 games, 4 weeks, 20 players) runs through fact builder and checker with the
  rejection rate reported per feature (target under 20%; above it opens a task, it does not relax the
  checker); cost per group per month reported against the $2 cap; product reads a sample of 30 published lines
  for tone against 4.3 and every hidden line. Gaps become `M16.8+`.
