- The landing hero, problem, proof, try, companion, final CTA and FAQ: verbatim, except rows 8, 20 to 23.

- Board: `Still settling`, `settling · 4/10`, the settling line, `+ N people who haven't played a rated game yet.`, empty board: verbatim. `Leaderboard` (h1) beside the `Board` tab is the accepted 05-design rule.
- `Rating` capitalisation: capital when it labels the number (chip, column, tile, `Rating 1512`), lower case in a sentence (`your rating moves`). The modules follow this; keep it.
- `game` vs `match`: `match` only appears in "match history", the client's own name for it. Correct.
- Role words print lower case (`adc`, `support`) in sentences and in backticks, the house style since M3. Not changed here.
